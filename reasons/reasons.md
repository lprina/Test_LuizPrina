Why Deploy a Spark-Kafka Integration on Kubernetes?
1. Scalability: Both Spark and Kafka are designed for scalability, allowing for processing massive amounts of data. 
Deploying them on Kubernetes ensures that the resources they consume can be easily adjusted to handle varying workloads.

2. Fault Tolerance: Kubernetes ensures that if any component of your Spark-Kafka pipeline fails, it will be automatically 
restarted, ensuring the continuous processing of your data.

3. Flexibility: Kubernetes’ container-based architecture allows you to easily swap out components and modify configurations, 
providing you with more control over your data processing pipeline.

4. Simplified Deployment: Kubernetes simplifies the deployment and management of complex applications, making it easier 
to maintain and scale your Spark-Kafka integration.
